Help & Guide

Complete Interaction Guide

1. Adding Media & Playlist

Click IMPORT to load video or audio files. This supports MP4, WebM, MP3, etc. Your files are NOT uploaded anywhere; they stay locally in your browser. Use the PLAYLIST tab (top nav) to switch between loaded files quickly.

2. Real-time Subtitle Editor

  • Spacebar: Play/Pause the video (when not typing).
  • Add: Click the "New Line" button in the right panel to create an empty subtitle block.
  • Edit: Type directly in the textboxes. The active subtitle (matching the video time) will glow.
  • Split: Hover over a subtitle and click the scissors icon to split it exactly in half.
  • Delete: Hover over a subtitle and click the trash can icon.
  • Smart Dictionary: Highlight a word inside your subtitle text, then right-click. A popup will show meanings and synonyms.
  • Focus Mode: Pressing Enter while a subtitle is selected will seek the video directly to its start time.
  • Filter: Use the "Search Filter" at the bottom right to quickly find specific lines.

3. Interactive Timeline

  • Zooming: Hold Ctrl and scroll your mouse wheel to zoom in and out.
  • Moving: Click dragging the center of any subtitle block shifts its time without altering duration.
  • Resizing: Click and drag the left or right edge of a block to trim its start/end time independently.
  • Seeking: Click and drag the Playhead (the needle) or click anywhere on the tracks.

Developer & Pro Features

For advanced features, see the README.md file in the project root:

  • Local PC Setup: High-performance offline mode.
  • Embedding: Use in Streamlit, Flask, or Web.
  • Packaging: Build .exe (Windows) or .apk (Android).
